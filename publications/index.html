Publications

Papers from my MSc and PhD. The full list with citations is on SciXplorer.

First author

  1. Presupernova O–C Shell Mergers Could Produce More 44Ti than the Explosion J. Issa & F. Herwig · ApJ 1004, 52 (2026)

    Across 3D mixing scenarios the pre-explosive 44Ti yield of a shell merger spreads by 4.8 dex, and for some it exceeds the explosive production and matches observations.

    DOISciXplorer
  2. 3D Macro Physics and Light Odd-Z Element Production in O-C Shell Mergers: Implications for 40K Production and Radiogenic Heating Inventories of Rocky Exoplanets J. Issa, F. Herwig, S. J. Mojzsis, M. Pignatari · ApJ 997, 314 (2026)

    P, Cl, K and Sc production across 24 mixing cases, and a 40K yield that changes by more than three orders of magnitude with the mixing.

    DOISciXplorer
  3. Impact of 3D Macrophysics and Nuclear Physics on the p-nuclei in O–C Shell Mergers J. Issa, F. Herwig, P. A. Denissenkov, M. Pignatari · ApJ 997, 41 (2026)

    3D mixing uncertainties change p-nucleus production as much as nuclear physics uncertainties do.
